# Paired PurpleAir event exclusion audit

This process finds elevated outdoor PM2.5 episodes where the paired indoor
PurpleAir sensor shows little or no response. It uses only hourly PurpleAir
`pm2.5_atm` to detect and score events. It does not read TEMPO, NAQFC, weather,
or model predictions.

Pair selection uses three existing `purple-air-pull` artifacts:

- `purpleair_continental_us_pairs.csv` is an untracked July 20, 2026 snapshot
  generated by `find_indoor_outdoor_pairs.py`. Its 1,427 rows are a greedy
  closest-first, one-to-one match of active/recent indoor and outdoor PurpleAir
  sensors no more than 100 meters apart. It is a project-generated snapshot,
  not an official PurpleAir pair product. It supplies non-school relationships.
- `purpleair_continental_us_sensors.csv` supplies the active sensor inventory
  used to match each school independently to its nearest outdoor sensor within
  1 kilometer. An outdoor sensor may serve more than one school.
- `smoke_plume_intersection/results/purpleair_indoor_school_sparse_wildfire_ranges.csv`
  is the school smoke-overlap list. Its 140 range rows contain 22 unique indoor
  school sensors.
- `purpleair_indoor_school_sensors.csv` is the larger validated NSD/FEMA
  classification cohort: `location_type=inside`, `k12_status=school`, and
  `is_k12=true` select 119 unique indoor schools.

The two school lists are unioned without conflating their provenance. Sixteen
sensors occur in both, producing 125 unique candidate schools. At the current
1-kilometer rule, mandatory permanent exclusions leave 104 active school pairs
using 81 distinct outdoor sensors; 18 school candidates have no eligible
outdoor sensor within range. The overlap list is only a cohort filter. The
detector does not read HMS geometries or use smoke labels when deciding whether
an event is an exclusion.

When `--include-downloaded-pairs` is supplied, every snapshot pair whose indoor
sensor occurs in the supplied PurpleAir histories is added as
`downloaded_history`. This includes the downloaded non-school cohort without
discarding the two school-specific labels. A pair still needs both histories to
be event-tested; missing histories remain visible in `pair_coverage.csv`.

The result is a training-compatibility screen, not proof that a sensor is bad.
A selected event can reflect strong filtration, a tight building envelope, or
different indoor operating conditions. Review the event graph before applying
the generated sensor exclusion list.

The permanent indoor training exclusions in
`../permanently_excluded_indoor_sensors.csv` are a separate mandatory input.
They are removed before PurpleAir histories or events are loaded. DSIS Room 2
(sensor 112546) was added on August 22, 2026 after event review showed that its
indoor channel was broken. PAPA JOE (sensor 71037) was excluded the same day
because its atypical indoor history is unsuitable for training and may reflect
commercial operations. Brooklyn Center - Inside (sensor 171059) was also
excluded because its highly episodic indoor PM2.5 likely reflects an indoor
process rather than outdoor response. Their raw histories remain unchanged for
provenance and sensor-quality auditing.

Isolated reviewed indoor anomalies use bounded half-open UTC ranges in
`../excluded_indoor_purpleair_ranges.csv` instead of whole-sensor exclusions.
Badger's Drift (sensor 106678) therefore excludes only its 1,256.5 ug/m3
reading from 2021-08-31 04:00 UTC through 05:00 UTC.

Known-bad outdoor readings are controlled by
`../excluded_outdoor_purpleair_ranges.csv`. Each row identifies an outdoor
sensor and a half-open UTC interval: `start_utc` is included and `end_utc` is
excluded. Blank bounds mean the full downloaded history. The detector removes
those readings before baselines, events, coverage, or sensor summaries are
calculated; it does not remove the paired indoor sensor. The same CSV is
used to generate the static masked-training assignments. Add future reviewed
sensor failures to this file instead of hard-coding IDs or dates, then rerun
this process before training.

The separate future-download outdoor failure screen is documented in
[`OUTDOOR_QUALITY_PERIOD_DETECTOR.md`](OUTDOOR_QUALITY_PERIOD_DETECTOR.md).

## Detection and selection procedure

First, deduplicate `sensor_index` in the smoke-overlap list and validate the
119-row NSD/FEMA school filter. Match each school independently to its nearest
outdoor sensor within 1 kilometer; outdoor reuse is allowed. Add, when requested,
the indoor sensor IDs found in the downloaded histories and join non-school IDs
to the July 20 pair snapshot. Keep every cohort source on the selected pair.
Sensors without an outdoor PurpleAir sensor partner are
written to `cohort_selection.csv` as `no_outdoor_purpleair_pair`; ambient-grid
pairs remain valid for their own workflows but are not substituted here. No
sensor is silently represented by ambient-grid coordinates.

For every selected one-to-one indoor/outdoor pair:

1. Read and deduplicate the two PurpleAir hourly histories by sensor ID and UTC
   hour. Conflicting duplicate values are rejected.
2. Remove outdoor readings covered by `excluded_outdoor_purpleair_ranges.csv`.
3. At each outdoor hour, calculate the median of the preceding 24 hours. At
   least 12 observed hours are required.
4. Mark an outdoor trigger when PM2.5 is at least 55.5 µg/m³ and at least
   25 µg/m³ above that rolling baseline.
5. Combine trigger hours separated by no more than two intervening hours. An
   episode needs at least three trigger hours.
6. Recalculate paired indoor and outdoor baselines from their common hours in
   the 24 hours before the episode.
7. Examine paired observations from the first trigger through 24 hours after
   the final trigger. At least 80% of those hours must contain both readings.
8. Measure the outdoor and indoor peak rise above baseline, their peak-response
   ratio, their positive area-above-baseline ratio, and the peak lag.
9. Select the event for exclusion only when all three response tests pass:

   - indoor peak rise is no more than 5 µg/m³;
   - indoor/outdoor peak-rise ratio is no more than 0.10;
   - indoor/outdoor area-above-baseline ratio is no more than 0.10.

10. Select an entire indoor sensor only after at least two independent selected
   events and a selected-event rate of at least 75%. This repeated-event rule
   prevents one unusual hour or one incomplete episode from removing a site.

These defaults are CLI options. Changing them creates a different exclusion
definition and should be recorded with the output. `summary.json` saves every
criterion used in a run.

The process detects elevated outdoor episodes from PM2.5 alone. It cannot claim
that wildfire smoke caused an episode without an external smoke dataset, which
this deliberately restricted process does not use.

## Static masked-training assignments

The same run creates a complete, exclusion-aware contract for school masked
training. For each school indoor sensor with history, it ranks every outdoor
sensor within 1 kilometer by distance and sensor ID. Reviewed whole-sensor and
bounded indoor exclusions become hard gaps. At each reviewed outdoor exclusion
boundary, the closest sensor that is valid for the complete interval is used;
the next-ranked sensor is used during a failure and the closer sensor is
restored afterward. Adjacent intervals using the same assignment are coalesced.

This matching is done only here. The training loader does not select fallbacks
or reinterpret exclusions. Newly detected candidates do not affect assignments
until they are added to a reviewed exclusion file and this process is rerun.
The existing `selected_pairs.csv` remains useful for event-analysis diagnostics
but is not a masked-training input.

## Run

From the `ag-transformer-model` repository root, use its virtual environment:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m purpleair_pair_exclusions.detect_pair_exclusions `
  --pairs ..\purple-air-pull\purpleair_continental_us_pairs.csv `
  --school-smoke-overlap ..\purple-air-pull\smoke_plume_intersection\results\purpleair_indoor_school_sparse_wildfire_ranges.csv `
  --fema-school-sensors ..\purple-air-pull\purpleair_indoor_school_sensors.csv `
  --sensor-inventory ..\purple-air-pull\purpleair_continental_us_sensors.csv `
  --school-pair-distance 1000 `
  --include-downloaded-pairs `
  --indoor-history ".\data\purple air\school_indoor_pm25.csv" `
  --indoor-history ".\data\purple air\general_non_school_indoor_pm25.csv" `
  --outdoor-history ".\data\purple air\outdoor_school\school_outdoor_pm25.csv" `
  --outdoor-history ".\data\purple air\outdoor_non_school\non_school_outdoor_pm25.csv" `
  --output-dir .\purpleair_pair_exclusions\results
```

Both history directories use the existing hourly schema:

```csv
time_stamp,sensor_index,pm2.5_atm
1693159200,12345,18.2
```

Paths may be repeated when histories span multiple directories. Files inside a
directory must begin with the numeric sensor ID. An explicitly named combined
CSV may contain multiple sensor IDs.

Do not assume that all 1,427 snapshot pairs have histories. The pair snapshot is
the outdoor-sensor lookup; the school lists and `--include-downloaded-pairs`
define the analysis cohort, while `pair_coverage.csv` records actual availability.
The 119-row `school_indoor_pm25/data/purpleair_indoor_school_pairs.csv` serves a
different, valid purpose: it is a school-coordinate manifest for evaluating
TEMPO and NAQFC at the indoor sensor coordinate. Its equal IDs and zero
distances are intentional, but it does not contain outdoor PurpleAir sensors
and is therefore out of scope for this detector. Also do not substitute the
364-row
`purpleair_continental_us_pairs_thinned_20km.csv` for either role.

## Outputs

- `cohort_selection.csv`: all candidate sensors, their source membership,
  paired outdoor ID when available, and explicit unpaired status.
- `selected_pairs.csv`: the explicit indoor/outdoor PurpleAir sensor join.
- `training_intervals.csv`: deterministic half-open UTC assignments for school
  masked training. One indoor sensor may have several adjacent outdoor
  assignments, and outdoor sensors may be reused.
- `training_intervals.meta.json`: schema version, matching radius, source and
  reviewed-exclusion hashes, generation time, manifest hash, and counts.
- `unresolved_training_intervals.csv`: indoor exclusion gaps and periods where
  no eligible outdoor sensor exists. No training interval is emitted for them.
- `pair_coverage.csv`: the selected pair list plus availability and
  exact indoor/outdoor hourly overlap for every pair.
- `events.csv`: every detected episode, its metrics, and its selection reason.
- `sensor_summary.csv`: repeated-event evidence for every analyzed sensor.
- `excluded_sensors.csv`: selected indoor sensors. Its first column is
  `sensor_id`. These are unreviewed detector candidates and do not alter the
  static training assignments.
- `summary.json`: counts, source statement, and complete threshold contract.
  Its `inputs` object records all selection lists, their counts, and every
  history directory used.
- `event_response_summary.png`: outdoor-rise versus indoor-rise result graph.
- `event_explorer.html`: self-contained interactive review of every selected
  event, with sensor navigation, metrics, and aligned hourly traces. Its pair
  metrics and aligned hourly traces. Click any hour in the chart—including the
  baseline before the event and the response period after it—to pin its
  timestamp and exact indoor/outdoor readings. The magnified indoor scale and
  zero, missing, range, and distinct-value diagnostic help distinguish a low
  response from a flat or potentially stuck sensor. The location selector
  filters both event navigation and the event table; location labels include
  exclusion counts and are ordered from most to fewest.
- `location_history_explorer.html`: full history for one location at a time.
  Use `Paired locations` for stacked outdoor/indoor histories or `Unpaired
  sensors` for downloaded indoor sensors that do not have a current pair. Use
  `Excluded sensors and ranges` for permanently excluded indoor sensors and
  outdoor sensors with reviewed exclusion ranges; the affected full history or
  bounded periods are highlighted and labeled with the recorded reason. The
  `Recent-data exclusions` tab is the subset whose `added_at_utc` matches the
  newest reviewed exclusion batch. Its
  searchable lists support names and sensor IDs. Previous/next arrow buttons
  review the active tab one location at a time. `K-12 locations only` filters
  each tab to indoor locations in the validated 119-sensor school cohort. Blank
  UTC start/end fields show the entire available history; `Apply range` limits
  the view and `Entire history` clears the range. Drag horizontally across a
  chart and release to zoom into that time slice; `Reset zoom` returns to the
  UTC field range. A click without dragging still pins an exact hourly reading.
  Keep the generated `location_history_data` folder beside the HTML file;
  histories are loaded per location to avoid loading every sensor into browser
  memory at once.

The tool never modifies a reviewed exclusion manifest or a checkpoint. Review
`excluded_sensors.csv` separately. If a candidate is accepted, update the
appropriate reviewed exclusion file and rerun this process; masked training
will reject an older interval contract whose recorded exclusion hashes no
longer match. Preserve the review date in `decision_date` and record one exact
UTC batch timestamp in `added_at_utc` for every newly approved row.
